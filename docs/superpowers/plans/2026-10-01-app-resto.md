### Meta documentation checked (WebFetch, 2026-10-01)

- Media insights, `GET /<IG_MEDIA_ID>/insights?metric=...`: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/reference/instagram-media/insights (also https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-media/insights). REELS metrics used: `reach`, `saved`, `shares`, `comments`, `ig_reels_avg_watch_time`. Response `{ data: [{ name, period: "lifetime", values: [{ value }] }] }`. Permissions with Instagram Login: `instagram_business_basic`, `instagram_business_manage_insights`.
- Account insights, `GET /<IG_ID>/insights`: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/api-reference/instagram-user/insights . `profile_links_taps` takes `metric_type=total_value` only, `period=day`, `since`/`until` as Unix seconds, answers `data[].total_value.value`, needs 100 followers or more, can lag 48 hours and answers an empty `data` (not 0) when it has nothing.
- OAuth endpoints and the 60-day token (already in `lib/proveedores/instagram.ts`): https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/business-login .

### Gaps in the documentation, handled on the safe side

- **The unit of `ig_reels_avg_watch_time` is not documented** (the page says "the average amount of time spent playing the reel"). The raw value is stored as it comes (`tiempo_promedio`) and `segundosVistos` reads it as milliseconds in one place; to confirm with the first real reading. If it turns out to be seconds the screen would show 0 s.
- **`profile_links_taps` is not the tap on the bio link.** It counts taps on the profile's address, call, email and text buttons. It is stored (`metricas_cuenta`) because the plan asked for it, but the screen does not show it: the "clics en tu link" of Métricas are the counts of `/l`, which is what the bio link really measures. Showing it would have been a number that does not mean what its label says.
- Day boundaries of the account metric (`period=day`) are not stated in the docs; each day is asked between its own midnights in Santiago, and the last three complete days are re-read every run because the figure lags.

### Deviations

- **Ports.** The brief asked for 54520-54529, but `wav-research-2` (a stack from another session) was already on 54521-54524. My stack `estudio-coni-b` ran on 54620-54629 (api 54621, db 54622, studio 54623, mail 54624, analytics 54627, pooler 54629) and the app on `E2E_PUERTO=3260`. Two harmless overrides were committed: `E2E_PUERTO` in `tests/e2e/entorno.ts` and the port of `SUPABASE_URL` in `tests/msw.ts` (otherwise MSW blocks the supabase-js calls to a stack on another port). `supabase/config.toml` was restored before the last commit.
- **Metrics.** One reading per reel per day (`metricas_reel`, primary key `pieza_id, dia`), upserted, so a retried run only replaces. Retries stay at the config default (3 attempts) instead of the `retry: 1` of the other schedules, because a day's read is idempotent. A dead token stops the reel loop at the first reel; any other reel that fails is collected and raised at the end, after the other reels and the account figure were saved (`enSerie`). The Instagram `consultar`/`leer` helpers were exported for it; the metric calls live in `lib/proveedores/instagram-metricas.ts`.
- **Comparisons.** `compararTemas` compares themes (pillars) only; the format group (avatar against her own clip) does not exist yet because every piece is an avatar. Nothing is compared until two themes each have 8 reels with a measurable saves-per-1,000. Then each theme shows its mean with a 95 % interval (Student quantile by Cornish-Fisher expansion, within 0.01 of the tables from 7 degrees up) and the best theme is set against the next with Welch's interval, with the sentence "se distingue del azar" or "no se distingue del azar todavía" and a caveat that themes also differ in hook and day. The "llevas N de 8" panel reads: the theme with the most reels (or, once one has 8, the next one). It sits right under "Lo que sirvió". The bars are per reel, scaled to the best visible, each with its number, base, pillar name and the n of its theme.
- **Consultas.** Only the moment is stored (no author, no text). Coni can undo a tap from the last day (RLS), nobody can edit one. The count lives in Inicio ("Llegó una consulta" and the month's total); Métricas shows the month and the week totals.
- **`/l`.** Names and title are constants (`lib/enlaces/perfil.ts`); the links come from `ajustes.enlaces`, each `{ id, titulo, detalle, url }`. **All four ship without an address** (no URL was guessed), and a link without a valid http(s) address is hidden, so the page is empty of links until Coni or WAV fills them in (SQL for now; Track A's Ajustes does not edit them). Clicks: `/l/ir/<id>` answers a 302 with `Cache-Control: no-store`; a person is counted once per link per day from `HMAC(HMAC(secret, day), address + "\n" + user agent)`; the secret is `SAL_DE_CLICS` (a random one per server instance when it is not set, which can only count a visitor once per instance, never link anyone). The hash is kept in `visitas_clic` only until the day after (`registrar_clic` deletes older ones) and only the counts stay (`clics_enlace_dia`). No address, user agent or salt is stored or logged. Not counted: `HEAD`, a missing user agent, known crawlers and link scanners (the Instagram in-app browser, which names itself "Instagram", is counted), `Purpose`/`Sec-Purpose`/`X-Purpose`/`X-Moz` prefetch headers and fetch metadata that is not a document navigation. There is no script on the page to prove a human, so "no-JS heuristics" are these header checks; a missing `Sec-Fetch-*` never counts against a visitor (older browsers). If counting fails the visitor still reaches the link and the failure is logged. `/l` is outside the proxy matcher: no Supabase call and no cookie for a visitor. Contrast of every text on the raspberry surface is checked in the real page (opacity 1, 4.5:1 or more); the small print is 15 px, not the mockup's 13.5 px.
- **Calendario.** Mes, Agenda and Ritmo are three links (`?vista=`), so each has an address and none needs a script. Mes shows dots only for pieces with an approved or scheduled time (so "Nada programado este mes." is true), Agenda also places pieces that have no date yet in the next free slots, the most advanced first, labelled with what each needs ("Falta tu firma: video y fecha"); a free slot links to `/ideas` (Track A builds that screen; it is a 404 until the merge). An approved time that no longer matches the schedule keeps its own row (settings never move it). Slots less than 2 hours away are not offered. Ritmo covers the last 4 weeks by the days of the schedule and suggests a change only with 3 or more weeks since her first reel, and 3 or more slots already past on that day, free in at least 75 % of them. Inicio got "Ver calendario" and the "Consultas" block; the bottom bar has no tab for Calendario.
